# GEN-90 Backup Generator - Weekly Checklist

Synthetic reference text written for the AlixAI facility-maintenance reference application. No real equipment is described.

## Weekly test run

Start the generator on no load and let it run for 20 minutes. Check that the oil pressure is above 2 bar, the coolant temperature settles between 80 C and 90 C and the battery charger shows float voltage.

## Monthly load test

Transfer the critical load for 30 minutes. Record the voltage, the frequency and the fuel level before and after the test.

## Fuel

Keep the day tank above 75 percent. Drain a small sample from the bottom of the tank every quarter and look for water or sediment.

## Decommissioning

A generator that is replaced must be drained, its battery removed and its asset record decommissioned by a supervisor in the Facility Maintenance application.
