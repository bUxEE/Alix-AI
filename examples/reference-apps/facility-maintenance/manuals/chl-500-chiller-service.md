# CHL-500 Chiller - Service Manual

Synthetic reference text written for the AlixAI facility-maintenance reference application. No real equipment is described.

## Daily log

Record the chilled water supply and return temperatures, the condenser pressure and the compressor current once per shift. The supply temperature set point is 7 C.

## Alarms

- **A12 High condenser pressure**: check the condenser fan, clean the condenser coil and confirm the cooling water flow.
- **A15 Low chilled water flow**: check the pump, strainer and isolation valves before restarting the chiller.
- **A21 Supply temperature drift**: the supply temperature is more than 2 C above the set point for 30 minutes. Check the refrigerant level and the expansion valve, then raise a work order.

## Monthly service

Check the oil level in the compressor sight glass, inspect the evaporator approach temperature (it should be below 3 C) and test the flow switch.

## Refrigerant top-up

Only a certified technician may add refrigerant. Record the quantity added in the log; a chiller that needs refrigerant more than twice a year has a leak that must be found and repaired.

## Parts

- CAP-45 Run capacitor 45 uF for the condenser fan motors.
