# Efficiency review — M5-09: final Lite model selection and 4 GB re-run

Every feature records this before it counts as done.

## Requirement
Finalise ADR 0010 (the Lite controller) and ADR 0020 (the 4 GB profile): re-survey current small open models smallest first, run the pre-registered pass bar of M0-13 (unchanged)
on every candidate under lite, balanced and 4g (emulated), and accept an ADR with the outcome the evidence supports. Authority never sits in the model (rule 5); a smaller model
replaces a larger one only if the whole suite passes (§8.3); the bar is not lowered to reach a default.

## No-model / deterministic option
The deterministic tier (typed forms, rules router, templates) is the Lite serving path today and costs no RAM. The cheapest lever tried in front of a model is the scope gate
`scopegate-v1` (configuration G, 30 microseconds a case): it answers 19 of 80 requests with no model call and lifts refusals to 10 of 16 for every model, at a cost of 3 to 5
routing cases. It cannot reach 16 of 16 (two refusal cases are about an offered object), so a model or an action-level rule is still needed for the rest. No model, with or without the gate, clears the bar.

## Decision
No candidate is the smallest that passes, because none passes: Lite stays uncertified and serves the deterministic path; the bar is unchanged; `default_for` stays empty and nothing replaces anything.
The larger rungs were not rejected for size: Granite 1B and Qwen3.5-2B miss the same lines as the small ones (refusal and injection) and spend 2.5 to 3.1 GiB. The smallest
model with usable routing is Qwen3.5-0.8B (51 of 54), and it fails the refusal line 0 of 16 without the gate. The decision layer (milestone D) has no evidence yet and no profile defaults to CLM-8B.

## Resource impact
Emulated numbers (lite 8 GiB / 4 CPUs, balanced 16 GiB / 8 CPUs, 4g 4 GiB / 4 CPUs, no swap): U peaks above; the cgroup sampler (1 s) ran for every session; no swap, no OOM kill and 0 major faults per second in any run;
Qwen3.5-0.8B grew its anonymous memory from 884 to about 1900 MiB of backend resident set on the first requests and kept it (same growth as M0-15, cause still not isolated). Latency was 1.5 to 2 times worse than M0-17 on the
same models during the lite sessions because other jobs shared the host (VM CPU busy median about 25% from this container alone); the 4g sessions ran faster. CPU, storage per model (file sizes above), power and concurrency were not measured.
Whole job cost: seven completed cold-start sessions of two runs each (about 4.5 hours of wall clock, one container at a time), about 3.5 GB of model files already on disk plus 688 MB for MiniCPM5-1B.

## 4 GB profile impact
Preserves the target, does not advance it. Granite 350M fits 4g with 1.2 GiB spare and no quality; Qwen3.5-0.8B has the only usable routing and sits at 2047 MiB (config A) and 2074 MiB (config G) against the 2048 MiB
line, i.e. no margin, and it is the same 0.8B that fails refusal and injection. The next two rungs do not fit (M0-15). The profile stays experimental and unpromoted; ADR 0020 lists what still blocks it.

## Re-evaluation trigger
2027-01-15, or earlier on: a sub-2B instruction model under a permissive licence appears on the first-party hubs; a new revision or quantisation of a rejected candidate; the scope gate or an action-level refusal rule is wired
into the engine; the conversation harness for conversation-v1 exists (then the gated-out suites run for any candidate that clears routing-v0); or a real 4 GB or ARM64 host is available.
