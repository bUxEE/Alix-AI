# Efficiency review — M2-15: live RAG measurement with LocalAI embeddings (emulated Lite)

Every feature records this before it counts as done. The full evidence, with every table, is in
[the RAG report](../evidence/m2-rag/report.md); the raw per-run results and RSS samples are not published.

All figures are **emulated**: one LocalAI v4.10.0 container capped by cgroup (lite = 8 GiB and 4 CPUs, no swap; balanced = 16 GiB and 8 CPUs; the cap was read back
with `docker inspect` and with `docker stats`, which `bench check` compares with the profile) on an i7-10700K host (8 cores, 16 threads, 64 GB, Windows 11, Docker
Desktop, no GPU), shared with other work, never a certified machine (§7.4). Latency is indicative and given as ranges over repeated runs. The model is the
registry's Granite Embedding 97M Multilingual R2 Q4_K_M (sha256 verified on the host and in the container). The controller was not loaded.

## Requirement
Decide, with measurements and not with the fake embedder, what retrieval the Lite and Balanced profiles default to: full-text only, hybrid (full-text + int8 vectors, RRF),
or hybrid + reranker. Decision rule (stated here while the work was under way, not pre-registered): no leak and no error in any mode; hybrid must not lower any retrieval metric below FTS-only's; its resident
memory must fit the Lite budget (`docs/operations/resource-budgets.md` 4.1: embedder backend at most 450 MiB, LocalAI service 150 MiB); its query-embedding latency must
leave the interactive budget intact (the controller's time to first token alone is about 3 s on Lite, M0-13). Also owed here, from M2-14 and the ADRs: the live
`--embedder localai` path; LocalAI's memory; query-embedding latency (the sweep never measured a model); `vector_min_score` swept on the real embedder; the reader-cache
hypothesis for the corpus-size effect on a small scope (ADR 0032); recall@10 of the int8 stored form on real vectors against ADR 0011's 0.95 bar and the build throughput (ADR 0011).

## No-model / deterministic option
Measured, and it is the baseline of every row: **FTS-only** over the same labelled corpus (50 questions, 35 to answer), no model, no vectors, no container. Recall@1 0.900,
recall@5 0.900, MRR 0.914, 31 of 35 answerable answered and every answer right, 1 of 15 unsupported answers, 0 leaks, 0 errors; 1.9 to 2.0 ms p50 and 3.0 to 3.1 ms p95 end
to end, 1,078 to 1,132 chunks/s to build, 348,160 B of index (3,380 B/chunk), 28 to 29 MiB for the evaluating process. It is the Lite floor and the degraded mode when no
embedder is resident (`no_embedder`). What it cannot do is the case for the embedder: the five cross-language questions (recall@1/@5 0.3 / 0.3, never above 0.3 at any k) and the
Italian questions (recall@5 0.833, MRR 0.833).

## Decision
The smallest candidate that passes: **hybrid retrieval with Granite Embedding 97M Q4_K_M as the default for Lite and Balanced, FTS-only as the always-available fallback, no reranker, `vector_min_score` 0 in code.**
FTS-only passes the safety line (no leak, no error) and is 25 times faster, but it cannot retrieve across languages (cross-language recall 0.3 at every k, Italian recall 0.833) and the product is bilingual; hybrid is the
smallest step that moves those, and it moves nothing else down (no metric under FTS-only's in any cell, 0 leaks, 0 errors). It fits the Lite budget with one exception noted below.

- **Cost accepted:** +45 ms a query, 440 to 454 MiB resident (538 MiB with long chunks), 8.7 s cold start (so the embedder stays resident, R3), 24 to 32 chunks/s to ingest (on the job queue).
- **Rejected:** the harness's lexical reranker (recall@1 0.900 to 0.829, MRR 0.933 to 0.869); a 64 MiB reader cache (1.2 ms for 140 MiB resident); the Q8_0 file (M0-13: no gain, +10 MB); a floor in code (below).
- **`vector_min_score` stays 0 in code.** Swept on the real embedder (0 to 0.9): nothing changes up to 0.6; from 0.8 to 0.9 evidence tokens fall 22 to 32% (11% at Lite's package) with no metric lower and one cross-language question
  recovered (non-monotonic: 0.85 best, 0.9 lower). 35 questions and one corpus cannot fix a number, and a floor is a threshold on one model's raw cosine: it belongs in the registry entry of the model (candidate 0.80), after a
  labelled corpus of a few hundred questions and a real controller confirm it. Not adopted.
- **Findings to act on (outside this task's scope, not fixed here):** (1) the LocalAI service process peaks at 160 MiB resident against the 150 MiB row of `resource-budgets.md` 4.1 (the embedder backend, 294 to 380 MiB against 450, has room);
  (2) ADR 0032's sentence needs the full-text term (about 0.08 ms per 1,000 chunks of other scopes; the reader cache is about 1.2 of 4.6 ms); (3) the registry should carry a per-model vector floor; (4) recall@10 of the int8 form is 0.972 to
  0.978 on natural language but 0.922 on pseudo-word text: repeat on a real corpus of 10,000+ chunks before ADR 0011's remedy is ruled out; (5) the extractive generator hides the retrieval gain (the same four questions are declined in every mode): the
  answer-level value of the embedder is unmeasured until a controller qualifies; (6) on this machine Go's `exec` does not find a bare `docker`: `--docker-bin` takes an absolute path.
- **Rejected measurement choices:** `docker stats` alone (273 MiB against the 440 MiB resident set: it hides the mapped weights); 100 questions for the rotating cache arm (too few to warm a larger cache: it would have rejected the hypothesis
  for the wrong reason); labelling the lexical-rerank run `synthetic: false` (it is a word-overlap score); writing a fake-embedder sweep into a localai result (the sweep now embeds with the run's embedder and says so).

## Resource impact
All emulated; Lite = 8 GiB / 4 CPUs unless stated.

- **RAM.** LocalAI with the embedder resident: resident set (two processes) **440 to 454 MiB** with short inputs (median 445), service 124 to 160 (median 150), backend 291 to 294; **538 MiB peak** with long chunks (backend 380, anonymous 264).
  `docker stats`: 252 to 283 MiB, 367 MiB after long chunks. Balanced container: 443 / 452 MiB resident, 264 to 268 MiB `docker stats`. The evaluating process: 28 to 33 MiB (56 MiB in the 30,000-chunk query phase). Nothing near the cap.
- **CPU.** The model took **350% of the 400% cap** during the 30,000-chunk build (three `docker stats` readings, 350 to 352%); a single query's embedding does not use more cores (Balanced: 42 to 44.5 ms against 43 to 46 at Lite). Power not measured.
- **Latency.** +45 ms per query (p95 +50 to 56) on top of 2 ms: 47 to 50 ms mean embedding stage at any corpus size from 1,000 to 30,000 chunks; hybrid p50 / p95 of a 1,000-chunk scope **50.7 / 57.2 ms alone and 54.3 / 68.4 ms in a
  30,000-chunk corpus**, a growth the fake shows too (6.2 to 6.4 to 10.1 to 10.7). Cold start 8.7 s.
- **Storage.** Weights 105 MB. Index: 3,380 B/chunk FTS-only, 4,056 B/chunk hybrid on the 103-chunk corpus; 2,058 B/chunk at 30,000 chunks (61.7 MB), identical with the fake. Results of this task: about 7 MB of raw JSON (the RSS samples are 0.2 MB), not published.
- **Ingestion.** 24.0 to 25.7 chunks/s at Lite (30 to 32 for short chunks), 29.4 to 39.4 at Balanced, against 4,153 to 4,599 with the fake: **42 s per 1,000 chunks, 19.5 minutes per 30,000**, computed 17 minutes for 25,000 and 2.3 hours for 200,000.
- **Concurrency.** One request at a time was measured; nothing under concurrent load.
- **Time of the measurement itself.** A labelled run is 10 s with the model and 1 s without; the live 30,000-chunk sweep 21 minutes; the quantisation check 90 s; a fake cache arm 35 to 60 s. `go test ./tools/rageval/` about 20 s.

## 4 GB profile impact
**Preserves it.** Nothing here ships: the changes are in a development tool, no binary, container or default changes, and `go.mod` is untouched. What it measured bears on the target:

- The embedder is **not free on 4 GB**: 440 to 454 MiB resident (538 MiB with long chunks) for the service plus the backend, on top of a controller that already needs 0.85 to 2.8 GiB resident depending on the candidate (M0-13), with an 8.7 s cold start. That is the
  measured reason `resource-budgets.md` R2 declares no resident embedder on `experimental-4g`: retrieval there stays **FTS-only at query time**, which cost 1.6 to 2.0 ms and 28 to 29 MiB in the evaluating process and holds the
  same safety figures (0 leaks, 0 errors) and the same answer figures as hybrid; the embedder runs only in a serialised ingestion window (24 to 32 chunks/s, 585 B/chunk of stored vectors, 25,000-vector ceiling: 17 minutes to rebuild).
- The int8 stored form keeps ADR 0011's bar on natural language (0.972 to 0.978), so the 4 GB profile loses nothing to the quantisation it already pays for.
- Not measured under the 4 GB cap, and not certified: this run used only the Lite and Balanced caps.

## Re-evaluation trigger
- **A qualified controller** (M0-17 follow-ups): rerun the same corpus with a LocalAI answer generator to see whether the retrieval gain (cross-language recall 0.3 to 0.6, Italian MRR 0.833 to 0.875 (Lite package) or 0.887 (eight items)) becomes answers, and to settle the vector floor.
- **A compact reranker model plus an adapter route** (the five-path allowlist would have to be widened by its owner), or a larger embedding model ("qualified larger replacement").
- **A labelled corpus of a few hundred questions and a real corpus of 10,000+ chunks**: the floor, the quantisation bar at scale, and live 20,000 and 50,000-chunk scopes.
- A LocalAI bump (`allowed_runtime_versions` lists only v4.10.0), a new embedding model release, a change of the query or answer defaults, the index layout (ADR 0032), the vector cache, or the profile package caps.
- Real target hardware (an 8 GB x86-64 and an ARM64 machine): nothing here is certified.
- Review date: 2026-12-02, or earlier on any of the above.
