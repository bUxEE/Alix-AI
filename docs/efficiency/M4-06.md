# Efficiency review — M4-06: optional voice (STT, TTS, VAD) on the Lite profile

Full evidence: [the voice report](../evidence/m4-voice/report.md). Every figure is **emulated** (8 GiB, 4 CPU container,
LocalAI v4.10.0, three cold-start runs per candidate, default backend settings).

## Requirement
Voice is an optional interface to the same session (§13): audio, VAD, STT, a finalised transcript into the normal turn pipeline, verified text,
then TTS. On the Lite profile (§7.1) it must add only "an optional small STT and lightweight CPU TTS profile" inside the Lite container budget
(5811 MiB peak, `resource-budgets.md`), without evicting the controller or the embedder (R3), for English and Italian. Pre-registered bars
(`protocol.md`): STT WER at most 0.15 on human read speech, real-time factor below 1.0, STT worker at most 500 MiB; TTS round-trip WER at most 0.10, time to
first audio median at most 1500 ms, real-time factor below 1.0, worker at most 300 MiB; no speech segment for non-speech audio; zero resident-role reloads.

## No-model / deterministic option
Text input and output, which the product always has (§18: STT unavailable leaves text input, TTS unavailable returns the verified text). That is the
chosen Lite behaviour for every part of voice that fails a gate. A deterministic voice-activity detector (energy threshold) was not evaluated: the
Silero VAD costs 0.9 MB of disk and 41 MiB, and the case against skipping it is measured, not assumed — every Whisper size turned silence and noise
into text on all 8 non-speech clips, so some gate in front of STT is required.

## Decision
* **VAD:** the ggml Silero model, always in front of STT.
* **English STT on Lite:** whisper tiny q5_1, the smallest candidate that passes; base is the quality step (its WER margin is 4.6 points against tiny's 1.2).
  No TTS is offered on Lite: the answer stays text. The shipped pack `en-stt` carries this.
* **Italian:** no Lite voice. STT needs whisper small (550 MiB, a Balanced worker; tiny and base miss the WER bar); no Italian TTS tried clears intelligibility.
  Larger TTS engines were not tried: the two Piper voices already exceed the 300 MiB row and larger engines raise it.
* **TTS:** none on Lite. Piper and Kokoro peaks are 354 to 796 MiB against a 300 MiB row that was an estimate; the measured numbers replace it.

## Resource impact
English input-only Lite stack (VAD + whisper tiny): 243 MiB peak and 182 MiB steady as two workers, 33 MB on disk, real-time factor 0.09, cold load 2.9 s.
With the Granite 1B controller and embedder beside it at five active backends: 0 reloads in 21 mixed calls, 3012 MiB total peak of 5811 with
the worst-case TTS included (Piper ljspeech 455 MiB in that run). At the shipped default of two active backends every voice call evicts the controller
(8 reloads of about 5 s each in 21 calls): `LOCALAI_MAX_ACTIVE_BACKENDS` must be raised with the voice workers. CPU: STT at real-time factor 0.09 to 0.16 on 4
threads; power not measured. Latency to first audio on Piper is the whole synthesis (the backend does not stream in v4.10.0). All numbers **emulated**.

## 4 GB profile impact
Preserves it. Voice stays absent from text-only profiles and from `experimental-4g` (its voice rows are "not allowed"); the finding adds no always-on service.
The 4 GiB container budget keeps about 248 MiB spare with the controller at its ceiling (`resource-budgets.md` 4.1) and the smallest voice stack peaks at 243 MiB:
no margin, so voice is not proposed there.

## Re-evaluation trigger
A LocalAI release that streams Piper or reduces the Piper worker below 300 MiB; a Whisper-class or Moonshine model with Italian under 500 MiB; Kokoro Italian
voices measured through the Python backend; a Balanced-profile voice measurement; a numeral-aware normaliser (the pre-registered one counts digits for spelled-out
numbers as errors); or 2027-01-04, whichever comes first.
