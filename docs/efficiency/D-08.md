# Efficiency review — D-08: live decision-layer benchmark and qualification (emulated)

All numbers are **emulated** (Docker cgroup caps on an i7-10700K host, Docker Desktop VM 31.3 GiB, no GPU) and indicative; nothing here is certified. The decision layer is optional, bounded, non-generative and non-authoritative: a scorer only ranks supplied authorised candidates and the deterministic controller re-resolves every selected id.

## Requirement
Decide, with measurements, whether a decision scorer (a qualified sub-1B scorer or CLM-v0.1-8B) may be served by the pinned LocalAI or a supervised sidecar, and whether the layer may be switched on for any profile. The default stays off unless measured latency, capacity or safety improves while task success, citation correctness, multilingual quality, fail-closed behaviour and the memory reserve do not regress.

## No-model / deterministic option
Chosen as the floor and the only option that could be measured. Configuration 1 (rules only, 86-case EN/IT corpus) answers 25 of 86 cases (29.1%) and avoids 23 controller calls (26.7%) at p99 0.5 ms and zero extra RAM. It cannot replace the controller (the gate regresses task completion, citation correctness and multilingual quality), but it is kept in front of it at no cost.

## Decision
The smallest candidate that works is the deterministic rule step; it stays on, and no scorer is enabled by default on any profile (ADR 0031). CLM-v0.1-8B is rejected for the Balanced profile by the resource gate and not measured for the higher-memory profile; the sidecar remains an opt-in for hosts with well over 20 GiB of free RAM (ADR 0030).

## Resource impact
Emulated. LocalAI v4.10.0 with the 1B controller resident: 1.4 GiB, cold load 13.9 to 15.4 s, warm call 0.35 to 0.74 s (p50 592 ms lite, 525 ms balanced), reload after LRU eviction 15.2 s, restart to ready 6.1 s. Sidecar: 16.4 GB of storage in `deploy/container/clm/data`, entrypoint re-hash about 3.5 minutes at every start (about 78 MB/s over the Windows bind mount), encoder load climbing to 13.4 GiB in 166 s. The balanced attempt drove the host's free RAM from 19 GiB to 2.3 GiB and the host later crashed; the cause was not established. CPU and power not measured.

## 4 GB profile impact
Preserves it: the Lite and 4 GB profiles never load a scorer, and the 16 GB encoder is confined to an opt-in sidecar that is OFF by default.

## Re-evaluation trigger
A LocalAI release that serves `/v1/systemone`, a quantised CLM encoder that fits Balanced, or a host with at least 48 GB of free RAM for the higher-memory run (D-H02). Also when `tools/decbench` can send an API key to a scorer.
