# Efficiency review — M0-15: resource budgets and the first 4 GB feasibility experiment

Every feature records this before it counts as done.

All figures are **emulated**: one LocalAI v4.10.0 container capped by cgroup (`compose.4g.yaml`: 4 GiB, `memswap_limit` equal to `mem_limit`, 4 CPUs) on an
i7-10700K host (8 cores, 16 threads, 64 GB, Windows 11, Docker Desktop WSL2 VM of 16 CPUs and 32 GB, no GPU used). They are not a certified machine
(§7.4), and other agents' jobs ran on the host during every run (host CPU median 69 to 86 percent), so latency is indicative. The core-process figures are
**dev box** (Windows, no container). "Memory" is `U = max(R, W)`: `R` the summed resident set of the LocalAI processes (anonymous plus file-backed mapped
pages), `W` the cgroup working set `docker stats` shows. Everything traces to the run's raw results (not published); [the 4 GB report](../evidence/m0-4gb/report.md) is the full account.

This task builds no product code. What it adds to the system is a **document of budgets** (`docs/operations/resource-budgets.md`) and an experiment, so the
review below asks the rule-16 question of what the budgets allow, and of the one experiment: what is the smallest model that is meaningful on 4 GB.

## Requirement
Turn the M0-13 numbers into per-component RAM, CPU, disk, token and latency budgets that every later task must respect (§7, §17.2, rule 17), seed the Lite
turn budget (2,048 estimated prompt tokens, 256 output, shortlist 5 with a ceiling of 12) with measured values, and run the 4 GB profile with the smallest
meaningful text-only model, no resident embedding or voice worker, `memswap_limit` equal to `mem_limit`, measuring steady-state headroom against §7.4
(at least `max(15%, 1 GB)`), swap and latency, then list what stops 4 GB from being certified. It is an aspiration: the report says what blocks it.

## No-model / deterministic option
Two no-model options were tried.

1. **The deterministic product path** (typed forms, the rules router, templates) is the floor in every profile and the only thing the 4g profile can serve
   today (rule R10 of the budgets). It costs no resident memory: the turn pipeline is 9 to 22 ms of one core (M1-14, dev box) and M0-13's keyword router
   routes 33 of 54 with no model. It cannot extract typed fields from free text, which is why a model is wanted at all.
2. **Sizing the budget by arithmetic from model cards**, which ADR 0020 did before any run (a table of 350, 600 and 1300 MiB for the three controller
   rungs). Measured, that arithmetic was **1.9 to 2.5 times too low**: backend peaks of 704, 1489 and 2498 MiB. It had the KV cache right (11.6 KiB per token
   measured, 12 in the ADR) and missed the compute buffers and sampler state (360 MiB at load, 940 MiB under load on the 0.8B), the mapped libraries and the
   125 to 146 MiB LocalAI service on top. Card arithmetic is therefore not a budget method; the budgets quote measurements and label what is not one.

## Candidates evaluated
The smallest meaningful model first. Under `compose.4g.yaml`, the 80-case routing corpus of M0-13, one request at a time, context 4096, grammar. "Peak U" is
all LocalAI processes (the 4g container budget is 2048 MiB); "backend" is the model process alone (the 4g controller row is 1650 MiB). Quality is the four
gated lines of M0-13 (bar 80/80, 49/54, 16/16, 16/16), repeated here only as a by-product.

Memory levers on the 0.8B, measured at context 4096 (1011 MiB loaded and idle): context 2048 saves 22 MiB, 16384 costs 145 MiB (11.6 KiB per token); an
8-bit KV cache saves 21 MiB idle; batch 128 saves nothing. None is worth a quality risk at this size, so none is adopted. The prompt-prefix cache answers
a repeated prefix in 0.14 to 0.34 s instead of 4 to 18 s, a latency lever for the prompt builder, not a memory one.

## Decision
- **The smallest meaningful candidate for the 4 GB footprint experiment is Qwen3.5-0.8B** (the 350M cannot route). It fits the 4g footprint and passes
  lines B1 to B4 of the pre-registered bar with 2462 MiB free (1438 after the 1 GiB allowance). It is not qualified and it is not certified: all four
  controllers are still rejected on quality (M0-13) and the 4g profile stays experimental (ADR 0020 option C).
- **Granite 1B and Qwen3.5-2B are rejected for 4g on memory** (2642 and 2714 MiB against 2048), not on quality.
- **The 4g profile declares one resident role, the controller** (rule R2): a resident embedder leaves no margin and one active backend reloads on every call.
  Retrieval on 4g is lexical at query time, the embedder runs only in a serialised ingestion window.
- **No optional model may silently evict the controller, retrieval or speech workers** (rule R3): `LOCALAI_MAX_ACTIVE_BACKENDS` must equal the number of resident
  roles, optional loads are admitted against the container budget or refused with `resource_exhausted`.
- **Budgets**: container budgets 2048 / 5811 / 12134 MiB for 4g / lite / balanced from `max(15%, 1 GB)` and a bottom-up allowance of 1024 / 1152 / 1792 MiB
  (M0-13's Lite bar of 5.8 GiB becomes 5.67 GiB; no candidate is within 2 GiB of either). Token caps are the M3-03 table confirmed against measured sizes, so
  `profile.go` needs no edit; real tokens are 0.49 to 0.57 of the estimate for engine proposals and 0.75 to 0.83 for plain text, so the estimator stays as it is
  (it fails closed). Latency lines are proposals (call p95 20 s, response TTFT p95 5 s, whole turn p95 35 s, cold start 60 s).
- Left to the orchestrator: ADRs for rule R3, the latency lines and the Lite allowance; a task for the `LOCALAI_MAX_ACTIVE_BACKENDS` guard.

Beyond the model table, measured on the 0.8B:

- **Prefill** 106 to 144 tokens per second (linear in the prompt, 9 s for 1,025 tokens, 27 s for 3,046), decode 12 to 18; the host's load moves prefill by about 20 percent.
- **Memory follows the prompt**: `U` 1012 MiB loaded, 1634 under the bench, 1942 under a 3,046-token prompt; the cause (prompt length or the grammar sampler on a
  248,000-token vocabulary) was not isolated.
- **Concurrency**: one slot, requests queue. One request 7.1 s; two at once 9.1 s median, 15.6 s worst; four at once 15.2 s median, 31.7 s worst.
- **Real engine prompts** (19, the estimates of M1-14 measured): 391 to 957 real tokens for proposals (estimates 787 to 1814), 249 to 278 for responses (330 to 369);
  about 36 real tokens (75 estimated) per shortlisted capability.
- **Storage**: the install before data is about 1.7 GB for 4g (image 829 MB uncompressed, backend 162 MB, model 533 MB, embedder 105 MB, binary 27 MB).
- **Core process** (one idle sample, Windows dev box, inference off): 23 MiB working set, 55 MiB private, 0.33 CPU-seconds over 20 s.
- **Power and temperature**: not measured (no instrument on the dev box); CPU-seconds are the proxy.

## 4 GB profile impact
**Preserves the target as an aspiration and makes the true distance visible; it makes nothing harder in the code, because it adds none.**

- Good news, measured: one small controller fits the 4 GiB cap with 2462 MiB free, no swap and no eviction, and the budgets keep that possible (resident roles
  per profile, no silent eviction, admission before exhaustion, token caps that bound the prompt and with it the controller's memory).
- Harder than the plan assumed: the card arithmetic of ADR 0020 was 1.9 to 2.5 times too low, a resident embedder leaves no margin, one active backend turns a RAG
  turn into 18 s of reloading, voice cannot be resident, and the 1B and 2B rungs, the ones nearest to passing M0-13's quality bar, do not fit.
- Twelve blockers (P1 to P12 in [the 4 GB report](../evidence/m0-4gb/report.md)) are listed with evidence and an owner. The first two are the decisive ones: no qualified controller
  exists, and the models small enough for 4 GB are the ones that fail the bar.
- Not evidence for certification: the host is a Windows Docker Desktop VM, the operating-system allowance is an estimate, and only the 80-case corpus was run.

## Re-evaluation trigger
- **M0-17** (grammar-constrained proposals and `bench-prompt-v1`): re-run the four controllers; if one passes, measure it under 4g against the 1650 MiB controller row.
- **M2-09 and M2-15**: FTS-only retrieval on 4g and a RAG answer prompt, which this review only computed (about 1,300 real tokens, about 11 s of prefill on the 0.8B).
- **M4-06 and milestone D**: measured sizes of voice workers, a reranker and any decision scorer, replacing the estimates in `resource-budgets.md` section 4.1.
- **A real 4 GB or 8 GB machine, ARM64, native Linux or rootless Podman**: replaces every emulated figure and the operating-system allowance.
- **A LocalAI bump** (every figure is for v4.10.0), a new Qwen or Granite release in the 0.5B to 2B range, or a different quantisation.
- **The first prompt-builder wiring of M3-03** (the engine adopting the profile): re-check the caps against its real frame.
- Review date: 2026-12-02, or earlier on any of the above.
