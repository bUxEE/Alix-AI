# Shipping policy

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days within Italy and 5 to 8 working days within the European Union. Express delivery takes 2 working days. Orders placed after 14:30 CET ship on the next working day.

## Carriers and tracking

Parcels travel with the carriers listed in the order confirmation. The tracking number has the form TRK-NNNNNNNN and is sent by email when the parcel leaves the warehouse.

## Customs

Orders outside the European Union may be subject to import duties paid by the recipient.
