# Support handbook

## Escalation levels

Level 1 handles questions about orders and accounts. Level 2 handles hardware faults and returns. Level 3 handles product defects and recalls and is reached only through a ticket number of the form TCK-2026-NNNNN.

## Working hours

Level 1 and level 2 answer from Monday to Friday, 08:30 to 18:00 CET. Level 3 answers within 48 hours.
