# Business returns

## Return authorisation for business customers

Business customers request a return authorisation through their account manager. The authorisation number has the form RMA-B-NNNNN, for example RMA-B-20817 for a pallet of Boreal switches returned by Wingtip Wholesale.

## Restocking fee

Returns of opened business orders are charged a restocking fee of 15 percent of the invoice value. Unopened pallets are accepted without a fee within 21 days of delivery.
